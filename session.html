How to set up

Lean the phone upright against a mug or a stack of books, front camera facing you, about an arm's length away (30 to 40 cm) at face height. That leaves both hands free and keeps the picture steady. For speech: your face in the top half of the picture, in a quiet room. Earphones help during the chat, so the assistant's voice is not picked up by your microphone. For the hand steps: rest your elbow on the table and hold the hand 30 to 40 cm from the phone, palm toward the screen, fingers up, whole hand in view; for the pinch, turn the hand side-on. If you have no stand, hold the phone steady with your other hand, never the one being tested.

What this is

A movement and voice check that tracks your own trend between visits. It is not a diagnosis and it does not measure force. Camera video, and the sound for the vowel and hand steps, are analysed on this phone and never uploaded. For chats, your voice goes to the speech service (ElevenLabs) so the assistant can hear and answer you; this app does not save the recording or what was said. Only a handful of summary numbers are shared with your care team, and only when you tap Share.
